# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Construction industry stakeholders: adult learners seeking credible entry into construction-management-track work (justice-impacted adults, returning mothers, veterans); community organizations, county colleges, school districts, and training providers pursuing workforce and apprenticeship funding; construction employers seeking prepared emerging talent.

## Product Purpose

Aedifica is a professional services platform for construction-related career development. It delivers Rebuild (a 12-week adult bridge cohort) and Launch (grant strategy and proposal-authoring services) as Year 1 offerings, with a sequenced expansion into curriculum, employer networks, and career exposure modules after outcome evidence is established. The site communicates institutional credibility, mission clarity, and design authority to partners deciding whether to engage.

## Brand Personality

Architectural authority. Editorial restraint. Institutional precision.

## Anti-references

Generic nonprofit homepages. Standard consultancy "our services" grids. SaaS landing pages with hero metrics and gradient text. Warm-toned career platform sites with stock photography and rounded corners.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility through restraint** — every design choice should signal institutional seriousness, not marketing enthusiasm.
2. **Show the pathway** — layout and hierarchy should make the sequence (Rebuild → Launch → Expansion) legible without labeling it.
3. **Editorial, not promotional** — typography and spacing carry the brand; decoration does not.
4. **Precision over decoration** — sharp corners, controlled color, deliberate whitespace over ornament.
5. **Earn the expansion** — Year 2+ services are presented as aspirational but unproven; the design should reflect that distinction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `useReducedMotion()` guard on every animated element throughout the codebase. Light mode only (no dark mode). Body text contrast ≥4.5:1 throughout.
