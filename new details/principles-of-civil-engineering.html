The Course

Engineering, taught the way engineers actually work.

Principles of Civil Engineering introduces students to sustainable development, engineering design, infrastructure planning, bridge design, financial evaluation, and the environmental, social, and decision-making dimensions of real projects.

Through lectures, case studies, and project-based learning, students explore how engineers and planners develop solutions that balance technical performance, economic feasibility, environmental stewardship, and societal needs. The course moves from foundational thinking to a defended final project — the same arc a practicing engineer follows from brief to delivery.

Infrastructure as the context

Bridges, water, transportation, and the built environment students see every day.

Sustainability throughout

Climate, environment, and social impact are woven into every design decision.

Real financial reasoning

Cost-benefit, life-cycle costing, and ROI — engineering meets economics.

Defended in public

Students present and defend a final engineering solution with professional communication.
