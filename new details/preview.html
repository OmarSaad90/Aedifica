The gap

The talent exists. The structured route does not.

New Jersey’s construction workforce problem is not only a volume problem. It is a channel-formation problem: available workers, CTE students, and overlooked adults often lack a structured, credentialed route into employer-ready construction-management work.

Aedifica’s launch-stage site should communicate a disciplined system rather than a generic training catalog: recruitment, funding, employer validation, instruction, articulation, and public reporting all have to hold together.

The Aedifica model

Workforce architecture, not isolated training.

The site organizes offerings as a connected pathway: launch with the services that can be delivered responsibly now, then scale expansion offerings after evidence is produced and published.

How the pathway holds

A cohort does not start until four artifacts are signed.

Curriculum without an employer commitment is a class. Employer commitment without recruitment and funding alignment is a sales deck. Aedifica becomes a venture only when the four-gate system is documented.

01

CBO / recruitment partner

A signed agreement defining recruitment, case management, attendance intervention, supportive services, and data responsibilities.

02

Funding / fiscal partner

A signed subrecipient, fee-for-service, or employer-funded agreement specifying payment triggers and reimbursement timing.

03

Employer commitment

A signed letter requiring interviews for a specified number of completers, with target role titles and wage bands.

04

Articulation pathway

A signed pathway with a registered apprenticeship sponsor, union local, county college, or employer training program.
